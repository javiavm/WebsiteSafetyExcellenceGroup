Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our services, process, and expertise.

What industries does Safety Excellence Group serve?
We specialize in semiconductor manufacturing, data centers, advanced manufacturing, and construction. We've completed 50+ semiconductor fab projects across 5 continents including the US, Israel, Ireland, China, and Japan.
What is SEMI S2 compliance and why does it matter?
SEMI S2 is the global standard for Environmental, Health, and Safety (EHS) requirements for semiconductor manufacturing equipment. Compliance is required by major fabs (Intel, TSMC, Samsung, etc.) before equipment can be installed. We provide SEMI S2/S8 compliance consulting and audit services.
How quickly can you deploy safety consultants?
We provide proposals within 48 hours and can deploy safety professionals on emergency timelines when needed. Our Quick Response Team (QRT) maintains surge capacity for urgent situations.
What certifications do your safety professionals hold?
Our consultants hold BCSP certifications including CSP (Certified Safety Professional), ASP (Associate Safety Professional), and CHST (Construction Health & Safety Technician). We match consultant credentials to your specific project requirements.
What is AEGIS AI?
AEGIS is Safety Excellence Group's proprietary AI-powered safety intelligence platform. It provides predictive hazard identification, automated compliance monitoring, and real-time safety analytics. AEGIS is exclusive to our consultants and clients.
Do you work with contractors or direct clients?
Both. We support General Contractors with safety staffing through construction and equipping phases (including tool install and AMHS), Subcontractors with compliance documentation and certification alignment, and Owner organizations with owner-rep services across the full project lifecycle.

ANSI/ASSP Z10 Sections 4.1, 4.2, 5.1, 5.2

Z10 §4.1 Management Leadership

Does senior management actively participate in safety activities (meetings, audits, incident reviews)?

Z10 §4.2 Employee Participation

Are workers empowered to report hazards and stop unsafe work without fear of retaliation?

Z10 §5.1 Hazard Assessment

Do you conduct documented hazard assessments (JHA/JSA) before work begins?

Z10 §5.2 Legal Requirements

Are OSHA recordkeeping requirements (300 logs, postings) current and accessible?

Step 3: Implementation & Controls

ANSI/ASSP Z10 Sections 6.1 – 6.5

Z10 §6.1 Resources

Is there a dedicated safety professional assigned to this project or site?

Z10 §6.2 Competence & Training

Do all workers receive documented, task-specific safety training before starting work?

Z10 §6.3 Documentation

Do you have a site-specific HASP or written safety program for this project/facility?

Z10 §6.4 Hazard Controls

Is there a written Lockout/Tagout (LOTO) program with trained, authorized employees?

Z10 §6.5 Contractor Management

Are subcontractors pre-qualified for safety and monitored during work?

SEMI S2/S8 Semiconductor-Specific

Is SEMI S2/S8 compliance documented and maintained for equipment safety?

NFPA 70E Electrical Safety

Is there a written electrical safety program (NFPA 70E) with arc flash assessments?

OSHA 1926 Construction-Specific

Is there a written fall protection program with competent person designation?

OSHA 1910.146 Confined Space

Is there a written confined space entry program with trained entrants and attendants?

Step 4: Evaluation & Improvement

ANSI/ASSP Z10 Sections 7.1 – 7.3

Z10 §7.1 Inspections & Audits

Are regular safety inspections conducted and documented with corrective action follow-up?

Z10 §7.2 Incident Investigation

Do you have a formal incident investigation process that identifies root causes?

Z10 §7.3 Corrective Action

Are near-misses tracked, investigated, and reviewed alongside recordable incidents?

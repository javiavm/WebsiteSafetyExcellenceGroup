The Stakes

Data Center Construction Runs on Schedule. Safety Incidents Stop It.

Per industry data, DC project delays cost up to $14M/month for 60MW facilities. Every safety incident triggers mandatory OSHA investigation and work stoppage. The math is simple: proactive safety protects your timeline.

30,000+ Arc Flash Incidents/Year

NFPA data shows 400 electrical fatalities annually. Fatal burns occur at distances over 10 feet. NFPA 70E compliance isn't optional—it's survival.

Fall Protection: #1 Citation—12 Years Running

OSHA cited fall protection violations 6,750 times in FY2024. Penalties reach $161,323 per willful violation. Elevated work in data centers demands precision programs.

79% of Outages: Untested Components

Uptime Institute research shows most failures involve components that passed individual tests but failed at integration. Commissioning-phase safety is where projects are won or lost.

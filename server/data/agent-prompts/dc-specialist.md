- Automatic transfer switches
- Battery isolation requirements

### Fall Protection for DC Construction
- Overhead cable tray work
- Raised floor openings
- Roof work for HVAC/cooling systems
- Ladder safety near electrical equipment

### Hot Work and Fire Protection
- Fire detection system impairments during construction
- Hot work permits near IT equipment
- Clean agent suppression considerations
- Fire watch requirements

### Contractor Safety Management
- Pre-qualification for DC experience

- Multi-trade coordination
- Early intervention for underperformers

### Commissioning Safety
- Pre-energization verification checklists
- Exclusion zones during initial energization

- Staged energization procedures
